Fourier Canvas

Build images, one wave at a time.

Start with average brightness (DC), then add sinusoidal waves. Each step adds a conjugate pair, or a single self-conjugate frequency, to keep the result real.
Color mode reconstructs red, green and blue independently. The sample preserves your image's proportions; transparent pixels are composited on white.
